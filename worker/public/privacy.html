Privacy

Meme Lab uses your submitted comment to retrieve and rank meme references. Matching and caption generation send the situation and relevant reference information to the configured AI service. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

Comments and feedback

Your comment and returned candidate IDs and scores are kept for 30 days to support feedback and evaluation. Landed or missed feedback is linked to that recommendation. Expired recommendations and their linked feedback are removed by scheduled cleanup.

Caption editor and previews

Caption editing and PNG rendering happen in your browser. Caption generation sends your situation and selected template to the configured AI service. Source previews may load from external media hosts, which receive ordinary network request information. Media rights are not established unless a licensed source is identified.

Optional forms and diagnostics

The optional newsletter and site feedback forms send the information you choose to provide to SaaS Maker. Their handling is described in SaaS Maker’s privacy policy. App diagnostics record bounded event and performance information; they do not include your comment in the app’s event payloads.
